MacroGrace is an independent software company. We build tools for collecting, ordering, creating, and understanding complex things.

Each work is made by a human hand. Modern tools help us finish it sooner. We prefer the substantial to the fashionable: clear systems, strong interfaces, and work made to outlast the season.
