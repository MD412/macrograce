Super Ergo Desk

The problem

Every modern desk makes people scrunch their shoulders. The standard desk height of 29 to 30 inches was never meant for a mouse and keyboard. The keyboard should sit lower, so the elbows rest at 90 degrees and the shoulders can drop.

Keyboard trays try to fix this, but the mouse area ends up too small.

The solution

A tiered design. A lower, full-width front tier holds the keyboard and the full mouse swing at true 90-degree elbow height. A higher back tier holds the monitor and everything else.

The lower tier should span the full width, or at least about 30 inches, so the mouse never runs out of room. The step between tiers should be around 3 to 5 inches, which also helps raise the monitor toward eye level. The underside of the lower tier stays thin, so knees and thighs have clearance. Ideally, the lower tier adjusts on its own, since the right height depends on the person and the chair.
