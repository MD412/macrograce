Super shoulder saving ergo desk

Two tiers. Keyboard and mouse sit on a sunken full-width shelf so your elbows rest at 90° and your shoulders drop. The top stays at writing height and tilts into an easel while the ultrawide keeps facing you.

Slide mechanism. A fixed steel arm on each side ties the front and back legs together. The arms stop behind the top's front edge. A three-part telescoping ball-bearing slide sits on each arm and the lower tier bolts to its moving rail, so the rails only extend under the tier as it comes out. Pull the bar under the front edge to move it; the orange lever on the right clamps it in place. Pushed fully in, the keyboard and mouse park under the top.

Everything is sized outward from the deskmat. Heights assume a seated user around 5′8″ to 5′10″; adjust the lower tier to your own elbow height.
